Saait: the design system

Saait is the Nepali word for an auspicious date. The whole product is organised around the date, so the system is too: a marigold dot marks a wedding date everywhere it appears, sindoor red is reserved for the one action that commits a date, and teal marks a provider the operator has verified. Everything else is quiet: white ground, ink text, one-pixel lines, no shadows.

Text on white uses Ink (15.6:1) or Stone (5.3:1). White on Sindoor is 7.3:1. Dark text on Marigold is 8.1:1. Red and marigold are never used to mean opposite things, and verified is never shown by colour alone.

Type: Mukta, one family

Mukta was designed Devanagari-first, so Nepali and English sit on one baseline with one personality. Weight does the hierarchy work; no second typeface.

Day cells are 60 px tall with a 12 px radius. The selected day inverts to Ink. A marigold dot under the number means a wedding date; it is also written out beside the calendar so the meaning never rests on the dot alone.
Dates are shown in the Nepali calendar first, with the Gregorian date in Small type where it matters (confirmations, SMS).

Messages the system sends

Saait: Urgent job. Wedding photo, Falgun 13 (Feb 25), 10am to 4pm, Butwal. Budget Rs 30k to 45k. First to accept wins. Accept: saait.np/u/8K2P
SMS to client, booking confirmed
Saait: Falgun 12 is locked with Shrestha Studio. Deposit Rs 8,750 received. Balance Rs 26,250 is paid to the studio after the event. Code SA-4471.

Copy rules: sentence case, plain verbs, the same word for the same action everywhere (lock, deposit, balance, accept). Errors say what happened and what to do. No exclamation marks.

Spacing, radius, lines

Spacing on a 4 px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 56. Screen gutter 20 px on phone, 64 px on desktop. Radius 12 px for controls and day cells, 14 px for buttons and inputs, 16 px for tiles and cards, 999 for chips. Lines are 1 px in Line colour; there are no drop shadows. Portfolio tiles are 4:5.
