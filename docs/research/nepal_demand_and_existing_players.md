# Nepal: demand side of hiring photographers / videographers / content creators (2025-2026) and existing ways to hire them

Research note for validating a location-based "post a job, local creators bid" marketplace. Compiled 2026-11-07 from web search. Method caveat: this session could not open most Nepali-hosted pages directly (network allowlist), so figures come from search-result snippets of the cited pages; where a snippet was ambiguous that is flagged. Most price data is from vendors' own marketing pages, not independent surveys; treat as indicative. Nepali fiscal year 2081/82 = FY 2024/25 (mid-July to mid-July). "Lakh" = 100,000 NPR.

## Key Question 1: How many professional and semi-professional photographers/videographers are there in Nepal?

### Takeaway
No census or registry gives a national headcount. The strongest proxies are: the Kathmandu Photographer Association (an umbrella body for registered professional photographers in the capital, membership requires a business registration and PAN), the National Forum of Photo Journalists (500+ members in 35 districts), and the "Nepalese in Photography" Facebook group (~294k members, overwhelmingly hobbyists). Qualitative reporting says the number of wedding photographers "multiplied" in the late 2010s.

### Cited Findings
- Kathmandu Photographer Association (KPA): non-profit umbrella organisation for professional photographers, registered with Kathmandu District Administration Office in 2008 (BS 2065); membership requires a business registration certificate and PAN number; regular membership fee NPR 2,500; runs a "KPA Wedding Photo Award" — [KPA](https://kpa.org.np/). No member count was visible in search results.
- KPA president reported as Hari Prasad Ghimire, a wedding photographer and founder of Photo Choice Nepal (report ~mid-2025) — [Photochoice Nepal](https://photochoicenepal.com/hari-prasad-ghimire-elected-president-of-kathmandu-photographers-association-kpa/).
- A regional "Dang District Photographers Association" exists, and a "Photographers Association of Nepal" is listed (unverified) in a Kathmandu directory — [Photopasal](https://www.photopasal.com/photographers); [NepalYP](https://www.nepalyp.com/company/39657/Photographers_Association_of_Nepal).
- National Forum of Photo Journalists (NFPJ), established 1997, has "over 500 members across 35 districts" — [Rising Nepal](https://risingnepaldaily.com/news/67123).
- Photojournalist Club (PJ Club), established 2004; its public general-members page lists roughly 20 names (not a stated total) — [PJ Club](http://pjclub.com.np/general-members/).
- Nepal Photographic Society: began 1987 as Amateur Photographers Association of Nepal, renamed 1992; no membership figure found — [ECS Nepal](http://ecs.com.np/features/life-in-a-click).
- "Nepalese in Photography" (NIP), a Facebook-based community founded 2009, reports "more than 293,800 members" on Facebook — [Nepalese Photography (NIP)](https://www.nepalesephotography.com/). An older (~10-year-old) listing gave ~115k, so it has roughly tripled — [HamroBuzz](http://hamrobuzz.com/595-facebook-groups-nepal/).
- OnlineKhabar (c. 2021): "the business has skyrocketed now and the number of wedding photographers has multiplied", with rising competition cited as a key challenge — [OnlineKhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepal-wedding-photographer-challenges.html).
- Nepal Trade Union Congress lists a Nepal Film Workers Union and a Nepal Cultural and Artist Union, but no photographers' or camera-operators' union — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Trade_Union_Congress).
- Upwork-related tracker lists ~921 freelancers from Nepal on Upwork (all categories; a ranking site, not an official count) — [UpworkMRR](https://www.upworkmrr.com/countries/nepal). A 2021 Oxford Internet Institute study cited 66,509 IT professionals/freelancers active in Nepal (all categories) — [Nepal Economic Forum](https://nepaleconomicforum.org/the-gig-economy-in-nepal/).
- Entry-level full-time photographer/videographer salary cited at NPR 15,000-25,000/month — [NecoJobs](https://www.necojobs.com.np/blogs/career-tips/videographer-salary-in-nepal); staff photographers NPR 25,000-45,000/month (2026 guide), freelancers NPR 10,000-50,000 per event — [KumariJob](https://www.kumarijob.com/blog/career-resources/how-to-find-photographer-job-nepal).

### Inferences
- The supply side is fragmented: one Kathmandu-level association requiring a PAN (i.e. formally registered studios) and no national register. Semi-professionals who shoot weddings without a registered business are therefore invisible to any official count; this is precisely the long tail a bidding marketplace would aggregate.
- Order-of-magnitude proxy only: NFPJ's 500 photojournalists is a floor for one niche; the 294k-member hobby group is a ceiling for "people in Nepal who identify with photography". Serious wedding/event operators probably number in the low thousands nationally (no source; inference from the ~60-70 weddings per auspicious day in Kathmandu alone, each needing at least one photo+video team — see KQ3).

### Gaps
- No membership figures for KPA or Nepal Photographic Society were retrievable; KPA's site must be checked directly or the association contacted.
- No count of registered photo studios (Department of Industry / ward-level business registrations) was found.
- No Instagram follower or hashtag counts for Nepali wedding photography were obtainable in this session.

## Key Question 2: What does photography/videography cost in Nepal (weddings, events, product, corporate, social content)?

### Takeaway
Wedding photo+video in Kathmandu commonly runs NPR 25,000-60,000 for a basic one-day package, NPR 75,000-150,000 for mid/premium cinematic packages and NPR 200,000-300,000+ for multi-day luxury; entry points as low as NPR 15,000-20,000 exist. Pokhara pricing overlaps Kathmandu (NPR 25,000-180,000+); the few data points for Butwal/Dharan are NPR 35,000-50,000. Hourly event photography is ~NPR 7,000-9,000/hour in Kathmandu. Corporate/product/social-content rate cards are thin: video editing NPR 1,500-4,000 per finished minute (corporate) and NPR 800-2,500 per minute (social), content-creator day rates NPR 12,500-24,750, and social-media retainers NPR 8,000-150,000/month.

### Cited Findings
Weddings (Kathmandu valley unless noted):
- Basic one-photographer package at NPR 15,000-18,000 — [Photopasal](https://www.photopasal.com/photography-package-for-wedding); basic full-day Kathmandu packages "start around NPR 15,000" — [Photostudioservice](https://photostudioservice.com/blog/basic-wedding-photography-price-in-kathmandu-nepal).
- 2025 guide: basic full-day coverage from ~NPR 20,000; premium Kathmandu packages include cinematic highlight film, drone and pre-wedding portraits — [BBM Productions](https://productions.blazebytemedia.com/blog/wedding-photography-price-in-nepal/).
- Average range NPR 20,000-50,000; "Silver" one-photographer 8-hour package from NPR 25,000; mid-tier NPR 40,000-60,000; luxury cinematic crews NPR 120,000-150,000+ — [Photoprintservice](https://photoprintservice.com/what-are-the-most-popular-wedding-photography-packages-in-nepal); [Artsy Photography](https://artsyghar.com/wedding-packages/).
- Wedding Story Nepal (Kathmandu studio) lists collections from NPR 75,000 up to NPR 300,000 (Platinum) — [Wedding Story Nepal](https://weddingstory.com.np/best-wedding-photographer-nepal); [Kathmandu page](https://weddingstory.com.np/wedding-photography-kathmandu).
- National range NPR 25,000-200,000; metro areas (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, Pokhara) banded at NPR 32,500-260,000, about 30% above the national average; peak months (Mangsir, Falgun) "command premium rates" — [MeroNajik cost guide](https://www.meronajik.com/prices/wedding-photography).
- Fotopasal (a Kathmandu wedding studio profiled by OnlineKhabar, c. 2021) charges NPR 60,000-100,000 per event, having started with an NPR 8,000 job — [OnlineKhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/wedding-photography-nepal-fotopasal.html).
- A Kathmandu studio claims 3,170+ weddings shot across Nepal (self-reported) — [ibride vendor page](https://ibride.com/ideabooks/vendor/2477352/).
Secondary cities:
- Pokhara: NPR 50,000 (essential one-day) to NPR 180,000+ (multi-day destination); structured data shows an entry tier of NPR 25,000 — [Wedding Story Nepal Pokhara](https://weddingstory.com.np/wedding-photography-pokhara). A Pokhara photographer listed at NPR 35,000 after discount; a Butwal-11 freelancer (serving Butwal/Bhairahawa) at NPR 50,000; a Dharan studio at NPR 35,000 — [WedTayari](https://wedtayari.com/service/photography-videography/).
- Kathmandu-to-Pokhara travel packages are sold as a distinct product — [Wedding Kathmandu](https://www.weddingkathmandu.com/kathmandu-to-pokhara-wedding-photography-videography-packages).
- No Chitwan or Biratnagar prices found; one studio says it serves Chitwan, Butwal, Dang without listing prices — [WedTayari](https://wedtayari.com/service/photography-videography/).
Events / hourly:
- Kathmandu studio hourly rate: NPR 7,000 for 1 hour (digital files only), NPR 9,000 with 50 prints — [Wedding Kathmandu](https://www.weddingkathmandu.com/hire-a-photographer-for-an-hour); "low-cost" small-event photo/video packages and graduation shoots are sold as separate products — [small events](https://www.weddingkathmandu.com/small-event-photography-videography-services-prices); [graduation](https://www.weddingkathmandu.com/graduation-photoshoot-kathmandu-nepal).
- International crew brokers quote Kathmandu corporate event coverage at USD 1,800-2,500 (one-person crew) or EUR 500-3,000+; these are foreign-client prices, not local market rates — [GetCameraCrew](https://getcameracrew.com/hire/corporate-event-video-production/kathmandu-nepal); [Beverly Boy](https://beverlyboy.com/kathmandu-videographer-services/). A Kathmandu videographer on Glimmer lists USD 300-700/day — [Glimmer](https://glimmer.io/u/wivuq-urawi-sojaq-enopu).
Content / corporate / social:
- Video editing: corporate NPR 1,500-4,000 per minute; social content NPR 800-2,500 per minute; reels USD 20-170 each (2026 guide) — [BBM Productions](https://productions.blazebytemedia.com/blog/video-editing-in-nepal/).
- Content-creator day rates on a freelance marketplace: junior NPR 12,510-16,785; mid NPR 15,640-20,980; senior NPR 18,455-24,755 — [Twine](https://www.twine.net/find/content-creators/np).
- Social media management retainers: Starter NPR 8,000-15,000/month (8-12 posts) up to Enterprise NPR 60,000-150,000/month (daily posting, full content team) — [Fossa Technology](https://fossatechnology.com.np/blog/social-media-marketing-cost-nepal); another agency cites NPR 15,000-45,000/month retainers with ad spend separate — [Arjan KC](https://arjankc.com.np/blog/ultimate-guide-social-media-marketing-nepal/).
- Influencer fees: nano ~NPR 15,000, micro NPR 25,000+, macro NPR 50,000+, mega NPR 90,000+ per campaign — [Krizmatic](https://krizmatic.com/cost-of-influencer-marketing-in-nepal/).
- Nepal's total advertising industry estimated at roughly USD 150 million (2025) — [Shashikant Shah](https://shashikantshah.com.np/blog/digital-marketing-statistics-nepal-2026).
- Whole-wedding budgets for context: "no single average", tiers of NPR 5 lakh / 15 lakh / 50 lakh in Kathmandu — [Kharchapatra](https://kharchapatra.com/blog/wedding-budget-nepal-tiers); big weddings NPR 30-40 lakh — [myRepublica](https://www.myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/five-reasons-why-you-should-opt-for-a-smaller-wedding-this-season); average NPR 10-30 lakh with ~half of spend on gold — [Hamro Niti](https://hamroniti.com/economicsofmarriageinnepal/); catering plus venue 50-70% of the bill — [CEO Tab](https://ceotab.com/wedding-nepal/).

### Inferences
- Price dispersion is extreme (NPR 15k to 300k for nominally the same "wedding package"), and vendors differentiate on team size, days, cinematic film and drone. That dispersion is a precondition for a bidding model to add value (clients cannot easily compare), but it also means "cheapest bid wins" would compress toward the NPR 15-25k floor and alienate established studios.
- If photo+video is NPR 40k-100k inside a NPR 10-40 lakh wedding, photography is roughly 2-5% of wedding spend; it is a discretionary line that families still rarely skip, i.e. a resilient but small ticket relative to gold and catering.
- Business (product/corporate/social) pricing is far less standardised than wedding pricing and is mostly sold by agencies on retainer, not per shoot; a per-job bidding board would be entering a market where few published rate cards exist.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of realised (not list) prices; everything is vendor list price or guide-blog figures.
- No Nepal-specific product-photography rate card found (per-SKU or per-hour).
- No per-shoot corporate videography local rate; only foreign-broker USD/EUR quotes.
- Chitwan, Biratnagar and Dharan pricing essentially undocumented online.

## Key Question 3: How big is the wedding/event industry and how seasonal is demand?

### Takeaway
No official count of weddings per year exists; the only estimate found (80,000-100,000 weddings/year, "NPR 100+ billion" wedding gig economy) is an uncited blog figure. Demand is sharply concentrated into priest-set auspicious dates in Mangsir, Magh, Falgun, Baisakh and Jestha (roughly Nov-Dec and Feb-Jun), with Falgun the single busiest month; Kathmandu sees ~60-70 weddings on a peak date and a photographer typically covers about six per season-date cluster. Poush, the monsoon, and Chaturmas are dead months. Passport photo demand is a steady but shrinking-margin baseload: ~1.36 million passports issued in FY 2024/25.

### Cited Findings
Volume / spend:
- Blog estimate: 80,000-100,000 weddings per year in Nepal; 200,000-300,000 temporary jobs in peak season; wedding gig economy "NPR 100+ billion yearly" — uncited and internally inconsistent, lowest-confidence source here — [Hamro Niti](https://hamroniti.com/economicsofmarriageinnepal/).
- Census 2021: 61.8% of the population aged 10+ is married; mean age at first marriage 19 (men 21, women 18) — [Economic Frontline summary of NSO census](https://www.economicfrontline.com/2025/12/population-data-of-nepal-2078-bs-2021-ad.html). DHS-based analysis: 81% of women 15-49 have their marriage registered — [Demography Matters](https://apoorvajadhav.substack.com/p/week-22-nepal).
- Nepal's wedding industry is "mostly made up of small businesses and individual vendors" — [CEO Tab](https://ceotab.com/wedding-nepal/).
- Event management: "more than 50 event management companies" operating in Nepal (undated); Apollo Events claims 500+ corporate events over 12 years — [Course Hero excerpt](https://www.coursehero.com/file/p6ctone8/Event-Management-took-a-big-turn-in-Nepal-when-two-event-management-companies/); [Apollo Events](https://apolloevents.com.np/corporate-events/). No market-size figure found.
Seasonality:
- Only 11 of 27 nakshatras are suitable for weddings (Nepal Panchanga Nirnayak Samiti official), so dates bunch and venues near those dates are scarce in Kathmandu — [OnlineKhabar, c. 2021](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/another-wedding-season-in-nepal-why-too-few-dates-for-too-many-couples-every-year.html).
- In Kathmandu "around 60 to 70 weddings happen on an auspicious day in a season, and photographers usually cover about six of them" — [OnlineKhabar, c. 2021](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepal-wedding-photographer-challenges.html).
- Most weddings fall in Mangsir, Magh, Falgun, Baisakh and Jestha; Poush avoided, monsoon quiet; the best dates go first — [Wedding Story Nepal](https://weddingstory.com.np/wedding-photography).
- BS 2083 has 41 auspicious marriage dates: Falgun 12, Mangsir 8, Baisakh 8 (Falgun "busiest by a significant margin") — [Mero Kalam](https://merokalam.com/blog/nepali-calendar-marriage-dates/). BS 2082 list points to Falgun 15-20 as the most-booked window; couples who miss Baisakh/Falgun shift to Mangsir — [Amar Nepal](https://amarnepal.com/topics/auspicious-dates-saait-of-nepal-marriage-bratabandha-pasni-griha).
- Date supply shrinks in years when Jupiter/Venus are combust or Chaturmas/festival months consume the calendar — [Katigate](https://katigate.com/en/blog/auspicious-wedding-dates-nepal-bibaha-muhurat).
- "October and November are Nepal's peak wedding photography season, and photographers are booked out months in advance"; book 2-3 months ahead in main months, a few weeks suffices off-season — [PhotoshootNepal](https://photoshootnepal.com/wedding-photography-price-in-nepal-2026-complete-guide/); [Wedding Story blog](https://weddingstory.com.np/blog/wedding-photographer-in-nepal).
- Photographers shoot through the season, edit through the off-season, and "the next wedding season soon arrives" — [OnlineKhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepal-wedding-photographer-challenges.html).
- Kathmandu Post (June 2025): pre- and post-wedding shoots, haldi/mehendi coverage and cinematic video are now standard asks, growing even outside city centres though "still emerging in villages" — [Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/art-culture/2025/06/28/how-nepali-weddings-are-evolving-in-the-age-of-social-media).
Passport/ID baseload:
- 1,356,887 passports issued in FY 2024/25 (down 116,438 y/y; >3,700/day), of which 573,774 via District Administration Offices, 266,308 at the Department and 278,802 at missions abroad; FY 2023/24 was a record ~1.47 million — [myRepublica](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/dop-issues-over-3600-e-passports-daily-63-90.html); [Khabarhub](https://english.khabarhub.com/2025/24/512837/). Issuance was throttled to 1,000/day during a 2025 booklet shortage, then restored — [New Business Age](https://newbusinessage.com/news/46053/nepal-limits-daily-passport-issuance-to-1-000-amid-supply-shortage).
- Free online tools now generate compliant 35x45 mm Nepal passport photos from a selfie — [Photogov](https://photogov.net/documents/np-passport-35x45-photo/).

### Inferences
- A rough triangulation (labelled proxy, not data): if ~60-70 Kathmandu weddings per auspicious date x ~40 dates/year gives ~2,500-3,000 formal-venue weddings per season in the capital alone; the national 80-100k/year blog figure is plausible against a population of ~30m with mean first-marriage age 19, but is unverified.
- Seasonality is binary: the same photographer who turns away work on Falgun dates has near-zero bookings in Poush/Asar-Shrawan. A marketplace's natural value is in (a) clearing excess demand on peak dates to second-tier shooters and (b) filling off-season with non-wedding work (business content, events, passport/ID), which is the segment with the least published pricing.
- Passport photography (~1.3m passports/year plus visas, licences, exams) remains a steady walk-in baseload for ward-level studios, but it is low-margin and increasingly substitutable by phone apps.

### Gaps
- No National Statistics Office or civil-registration count of annual marriages was found; the DHS registration rate suggests civil registers would understate anyway.
- No data on Dashain/Tihar-specific photo demand, graduation-season volumes, or bratabandha/pasni counts.
- No quantified off-season revenue drop for studios; only qualitative statements.

## Key Question 4: How do clients currently find and book photographers, and which Nepali platforms/directories/agencies already serve this?

### Takeaway
Discovery is Instagram portfolios, Facebook pages, word of mouth, wedding planners and the studio's own website; booking is by phone/WhatsApp/Messenger with an eSewa/Khalti advance. Several Nepali directory-style sites exist (Bihe Bazaar, WedTayari, Photopasal, MeroNajik, Nepal Photo Hub, Wedding Kathmandu) but all are lead-generation listings: none takes the transaction and none runs client-posted jobs with competitive bids. Horizontal local freelance boards (MeroKaam, Yaha Chha, Swadhin, Freelanced Nepal) are small/beta. Sajilo Sewa, the one scaled local on-demand services app, does not list photographers. International marketplaces (Upwork, Twine, Book An Artist, Paydesk, Journalist.net, MyWed, WedMeGood) list a few hundred Nepali shooters but are built for foreign clients.

### Cited Findings
Discovery channels:
- Instagram is "the primary portfolio platform for Nepal's photography market"; studio sites built locally take online bookings with eSewa and Khalti advance payments — [WebApp Nepal](https://webappnepal.com/service/photography).
- A Kathmandu studio takes bookings "by text, phone, or email" and builds custom packages per budget — [RPStudios](https://rpstudios19.mypixieset.com/pricing/).
- Social-media-driven wedding trends (choreographed entries, pre-wedding shoots) are "curated partly for online visibility" — [Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/art-culture/2025/06/28/how-nepali-weddings-are-evolving-in-the-age-of-social-media).

- Foodmandu: ~1,300-2,000 orders/day, seven cities (figures from 2020-2025 pieces) — [The Record](https://www.recordnepal.com/the-business-of-delivering-food); [Motar](https://motar-company.com.np/foodmandu-story-nepal-food-delivery/). Pathao Food launched in Nepal 2 Oct 2020 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathao).

### Inferences
- There is no live Nepali product doing reverse-auction job posting for creative services. Existing wedding directories monetise listings and hand off the deal to phone/Messenger; the closest functional analogue (client posts job, photographers respond) is Book An Artist, which is foreign-run and not localised to NPR/eSewa.
- Sajilo Sewa proves Kathmandu/Pokhara/Butwal consumers will book a vetted stranger through an app for a ~NPR 1-5k home job with a warranty; photography (NPR 7k-100k, taste-dependent, date-critical) is a harder trust problem, but the behaviour exists.
- Bihe Bazaar's 313 Instagram followers after launching an app suggests listing-only wedding marketplaces have struggled to get demand-side traction; vendors already have Instagram and do not need another brochure. The unmet need is on the transaction/trust side (deposits, date-holds, dispute handling), not discovery.

### Gaps

- Member counts for any Nepali wedding-photographer hiring Facebook group were not found; a logged-in Facebook search is needed.

- Rojgari, Kaam Nepal, Daraz Services, Hamro Patro services: no results returned linking them to photographer hiring.

## Key Question 5: Smartphone/internet penetration, digital payments, and willingness to pay for services online vs cash

### Takeaway
Infrastructure is sufficient for an app-based marketplace in cities: mobile broadband penetration 95% of population (subscriptions), ~16.5 million unique internet users (~56%), ~14-15 million social-media identities, 29 million mobile-banking and ~28 million wallet accounts, and electronic payments of NPR 98.4 trillion in FY 2024/25 (+71% y/y) driven by QR. But cash remains dominant for consumer purchases: small surveys put cash-on-delivery at >50% of e-commerce orders, and the gig/platform economy lacks a legal framework.

### Cited Findings
Connectivity:
- NTA FY 2082/83: broadband penetration 148.64%; fixed broadband 53.55%; mobile broadband 95.09% (4G 92.52%, 3G 2.57%); voice telephony 104.16% — [NepaliTelecom on NTA report](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2026/11/nepal-broadband-penetration-reaches-148-64-in-fy-2082-83.html); NTA MIS PDFs — [Ashadh 2082](https://nta.gov.np/uploads/contents/MIS_%20Ashadh_2082.pdf); [Ashadh 2081](https://nta.gov.np/uploads/contents/MIS%20Report_2081%20Ashadh.pdf). Penetration counts subscriptions and exceeds 100% because of multi-SIM — [NepaliTelecom](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2017/07/mobile-internet-broadband-penetration-nepal.html).
- DataReportal Digital 2025 Nepal: 16.5 million internet users (Jan 2025, -0.2% y/y); 14.3 million social-media user identities; 39.0 million cellular connections (132% of population) — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-nepal). Late-2025/2026 update relayed by secondary sources: 16.6 million internet users (~56% penetration), 14.8 million Facebook users, 11.0 million Messenger, 4.35 million Instagram ad reach — [NepaliTelecom](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2026/05/only-56-percent-use-intermet-in-nepal-sharp-reality-vs-expection-in-datareportal-figures.html); [Shashikant Shah](https://shashikantshah.com.np/blog/digital-marketing-statistics-nepal-2026). NapoleonCat puts Facebook addressable audience at ~16.5 million in 2025 — [NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/facebook-users-in-nepal/2025/06/).
- TikTok user estimates conflict (2.2m, 3.5m, "8m+"); treat as unreliable — [Arjan KC](https://arjankc.com.np/blog/ultimate-guide-social-media-marketing-nepal/); [Digital Gurkha](https://digitalgurkha.com/blog/social-media-marketing-in-nepal/).

- Student survey of Daraz users (c. 2020): 53% prefer cash on delivery, 38% digital, 9% either — [Theseus thesis](https://www.theseus.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/353802/Pathak_Chiranjivi.pdf?sequence=2&isAllowed=y). Daraz was 100% COD in 2018 and targeted 50% digital by 2019 — [Kathmandu Post 2018](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2018/11/08/the-e-commerce-market-in-nepal-is-worth-25-million).
- 2026 commentary: despite the 71% payments surge, "many SMEs still report COD above 50% of orders" — [Arjan KC](https://arjankc.com.np/blog/nepal-digital-economy-report-2026-preview/). Nepal described as "historically a cash-first economy" with COD the vast majority of e-commerce — [Gurkha Tech](https://gurkhatech.com/daraz-nepal-ecommerce-case-study/).
- Studios already request eSewa/Khalti advances for bookings — [WebApp Nepal](https://webappnepal.com/service/photography); Freelanced Nepal and Sajilo Sewa both settle via eSewa/Khalti/bank — [Founders Nepal](https://foundersnepal.com/product/freelanced-nepal).

### Inferences
- The payment rail problem is solved for urban users (QR + wallets are habitual); the behavioural gap is trusting a platform to hold money for a future-dated, taste-based service. A deposit/escrow via eSewa or Khalti that mirrors the existing "advance to the studio" habit is the lowest-friction design.
- Unique internet users (~16.5m) being far below subscription counts means the addressable client base is urban, young and concentrated in Kathmandu valley, Pokhara and Terai towns, which is where the pricing data also exists.

### Gaps

- No survey on willingness to book a photographer (as opposed to a plumber or food) through an app.

## Key Question 6: What do small studios actually earn and what kills them?

### Takeaway
Independent reporting (Rising Nepal; Kathmandu Post 2018) describes a steady decline of walk-in photo studios nationwide, blamed on smartphones and digital processes, with studio owners in eastern Nepal exiting over "unsustainable income levels" and the legacy Photo Concern shrinking from 180+ staff to 40. Wedding/event photographers, by contrast, earn NPR 10,000-50,000 per event (freelance) or NPR 60,000-100,000 per event (established Kathmandu studio) but face hard seasonality, "multiplied" competition, clients who undervalue fees and low social respect. No audited studio P&L data exists.

### Cited Findings
- "Photo studios are witnessing a steady decline in business across Nepal", operators cite widespread digital technology and smartphones; an eastern-Nepal lab says studio owners are leaving due to "unsustainable income levels" — [Rising Nepal](https://risingnepaldaily.com/news/67505).
- Photo Concern (iconic Kathmandu studio) fell from over 180 staff to 40 and is "the only legacy studio that remains" (2018) — [Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/miscellaneous/2018/11/24/still-in-focus).
- Vendor forecast: digitisation of paper processes threatens passport/visa-photo businesses; recommends online booking and mobile studios — [Photostudio Kathmandu](https://photostudiokathmandu.com/future-of-photography-in-nepal).
- Wedding photographers' stated challenges (c. 2021): standing out among multiplied competitors, meeting international standards, getting clients to value fees, low respect from guests, and season/edit cycle leaving no slack — [OnlineKhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepal-wedding-photographer-challenges.html).
- Fotopasal grew from an NPR 8,000 first job to NPR 60,000-100,000 per event (c. 2021) — [OnlineKhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/wedding-photography-nepal-fotopasal.html).
- Freelancers earn NPR 10,000-50,000 per event "especially during the wedding and festival seasons"; staff NPR 25,000-45,000/month (2026) — [KumariJob](https://www.kumarijob.com/blog/career-resources/how-to-find-photographer-job-nepal); entry-level NPR 15,000-25,000/month — [NecoJobs](https://www.necojobs.com.np/blogs/career-tips/videographer-salary-in-nepal).
- Peak-month premiums exist (Mangsir, Falgun) — [MeroNajik](https://www.meronajik.com/prices/wedding-photography); off-season bookings need only a few weeks' notice — [Wedding Story blog](https://weddingstory.com.np/blog/wedding-photographer-in-nepal).
- Wedding/event work called "one of the most popular and lucrative sources of income" for photographers (promotional source) — [Photostudio Kathmandu careers](https://photostudiokathmandu.com/careers-in-photography-new-job-opportunities-for-nepali-youth).
- Gig workers broadly lack legal protection; a rider's self-immolation in 2024 put a spotlight on unprotected platform workers — [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/ride-sharing-riders-self-immolation-death-shines-a-light-on-nepals-unprotected-gig-workers/); [B360](https://www.b360nepal.com/detail/23285/legal-protections-for-gig-economy-workers-in-nepal-navigating-current-gaps-and-potential-reforms-2024-Aug-26-009900).

### Inferences
- Two different businesses are conflated under "photo studio": (1) ward-level walk-in studios living on passport/ID prints and portraits, which are being killed by smartphones and online passport-photo tools, and (2) wedding/event/content shooters, whose problem is not demand but its concentration into ~40 dates a year and price competition from a swollen semi-pro pool. A bidding marketplace speaks to (2)'s off-season problem and to (1)'s need for new revenue, but "cheapest bid wins" would accelerate the price competition that group (2) already names as its main pain.
- Rough earnings proxy (not data): a freelancer covering ~6 weddings per peak cluster at NPR 10-50k each across 3-4 clusters a year would gross roughly NPR 2-10 lakh/year from weddings alone before gear, assistants and editing, which is consistent with the NPR 25-45k/month staff salary band.

### Gaps
- No quantified studio revenue, margin or closure counts (ward business registrations, Department of Industry) were found.
- The Rising Nepal and OnlineKhabar articles could not be opened in full; quotes above are from snippets, and the OnlineKhabar pieces are c. 2021.
- No data on how smartphone cameras have changed portrait/event demand since 2021, or on the share of passport photos now taken on phones.
