# Saait terms, privacy and chat rules (plain-language draft)

Status: draft for the lawyer, 2026-11-09. Written in the language the product uses so the same words appear on the sign-up agreement screen (Figma J2), the terms page (J3) and inside the app. Nothing here is legal advice; the lawyer confirms each section against the Electronic Transactions Act, the E-Commerce Act, the Individual Privacy Act 2075 and Nepal Rastra Bank rules before launch.

Every account accepts this on sign-up with one checkbox, after a screen that shows the seven rules below in full. Changes are announced in the app 30 days before they apply.

## 1. The seven rules everyone agrees to

1. **Messages are checked before delivery.** Software scans every message, image and voice note for phone numbers, payment QR codes, bank or wallet details and links to other apps, before the other person sees it. A message that breaks the rule is shown to you as "Not sent" with the reason, and you can ask for a review. Repeated attempts pause chat and freeze rewards. Only flagged messages are read by Saait staff.
2. **Phone numbers are shared 48 hours before the event.** Until then, you talk through Saait chat. The number appears on the booking page only, for that booking, and the reveal is logged.
3. **The deposit goes through Saait. The balance goes to a verified account.** Pay or get paid only as the booking page shows. A QR code or account number sent in chat is never from Saait and is always blocked. Saait's guarantee (refund or replacement) covers only bookings paid this way.
4. **Rewards are credit and points, not cash.** They pay Saait fees and plans only, expire after 12 months, cannot be withdrawn, transferred or sold, and are removed for a confirmed off-platform deal, a no-show or fraud. Saait can change earn rates with 30 days' notice; earned balances are honoured.
5. **Location is used once, never stored.** Your position is used to find who can reach the venue and is discarded. Only the venue pin of a booking you lock is kept, with that booking.
6. **Verification.** Businesses and freelancers complete identity and payout-account checks before they can take work. Saait can ask for documents again and can suspend an account that fails them.
7. **Your data.** Chat is kept for 12 months after the event, then deleted. Booking and payment records are kept as tax law requires. You can download or delete your account data from Settings; deletion keeps only what the law requires.

## 2. Terms of use

- Saait is a booking platform. The contract for the shoot is between the client and the business; Saait holds the deposit, guarantees the booking as described in section 4, and is not the photographer.
- Accounts are personal. One person, one account; a business account may have several staff logins.
- Clients must be 18 or older. Freelancers and business owners must be 18 or older and legally able to work in Nepal.
- You may not use Saait to find someone and then take the job elsewhere. Doing so forfeits rewards, lowers placement and, when repeated, closes the account. Saait never fines or charges for this.
- Reviews must be about a booking that happened on Saait. Fake or traded reviews are removed and penalised.

## 3. Chat rules

- Blocked: phone numbers in any form (digits, Devanagari, spelled out, split across messages), emails, links, @handles, other messaging apps by name, eSewa, Khalti and bank details, any image containing a QR code or barcode, screenshots of payment apps, and voice notes before a deposit is paid.
- Allowed: anything about the shoot. Dates, hours, venues, shot lists, prices, custom offers and portfolio links hosted on Saait.
- Checked by: software layers described in the proposal (normalise, rules, thread context, an intent classifier, image and QR scanning, voice transcription, behaviour signals). Blocked messages are stored for the review and for training the checker.
- "This isn't contact info" sends a blocked message to Saait staff. A cleared message is delivered and the strike removed.
- Strikes in 30 days: 1st notice, 2nd flag, 3rd 24-hour chat pause with points frozen. A confirmed deliberate leak resets the reward level.

## 4. Payments and the guarantee

- The client pays a 25% deposit plus the booking fee (4%, Rs 500 to Rs 3,000, VAT-inclusive) through Saait.
- Saait releases the deposit to the business after the event, less withholding tax as the law requires, within 7 days.
- The client pays the balance to the business directly, to the payout account Saait verified, and marks it paid in the app.
- If the business cancels or does not appear, Saait provides a verified replacement or refunds the deposit and fee in full.
- Businesses pay crew directly to the crew member's verified account; Saait records the job.
- Refunds follow the cancellation policy shown at the time of booking, which is saved with the booking.

## 5. Rewards

- Clients earn Saait credit in rupees, spent only on booking fees: 25% of the fee after a completed booking, Rs 100 for a review, Rs 300 for each side of a referral, Rs 200 for a confirmed report of an outside-payment request. Credit is applied automatically at checkout and expires 12 months after it is earned.
- Businesses and freelancers earn points (job +100, on time +30, 5-star +20, urgent +50, 90 clean days +50; no-show −200; confirmed off-platform deal resets the level). Levels: New 0, Trusted 300, Proven 1,000, Top 2,500. Points can buy plan months (1,500) and featured dates (3,000).
- Credit and points have no cash value, are not stored money and cannot be transferred. Saait may cap monthly rewards and will say so in the app.

## 6. Privacy

- What we collect: phone number, name, the venue pin of locked bookings, booking and payment records, chat, device information, and for providers the KYC documents and payout account.
- What we never store: your live position, your contacts, your payment card.
- Who sees what: the other side of a booking sees your name and, 48 hours before the event, your number. Businesses see a freelancer's alias, work and record; the name and number only after hiring. Staff see KYC documents, flagged chat and disputes.
- Where it lives: servers outside Nepal until a Nepal data-centre option exists; the lawyer confirms what the Individual Privacy Act requires.
- SMS: urgent broadcasts and booking alerts only; opt out of urgent alerts in Settings.
- Your rights: see, download, correct and delete your data from Settings, and ask a human at privacy@saait.app.

## 7. For freelancers

- Your profile shows an alias, your work, your reliability record and your level. Your name, face and number stay private until a business hires you through Saait. Portfolio images with watermarks, names or numbers are rejected.
- The business pays you directly to your verified account; Saait records the job and your rewards.
- You can open a public business profile when you reach Top, at half price.

## 8. For businesses

- Plans are monthly, prepaid, and include unlimited crew hires through Saait with no per-hire fee. Cancel any time; the plan runs to the end of the month.
- Your profile must show your real business name, registration and location. Clients see it before booking.
- Your reliability record and level are public. No-shows and confirmed off-platform deals lower them.

## 9. Changes, disputes and contact

- Saait can change these terms with 30 days' notice in the app. Continuing to use Saait after that is acceptance; you may close your account before.
- Disputes go first to Saait support, then to mediation in Kathmandu, under the law of Nepal.
- Contact: support@saait.app, privacy@saait.app, and the address shown on the website.
